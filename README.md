Exactly one emoji: 💐 — a bouquet, flowers offered as a gift. A stranger glancing at a phone screen reads it at once as a kindness deliberately given: someone handing over something beautiful with no words attached, warm and gently generous, universal across ages and backgrounds, and warm without intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation. It is not the first smiley that comes to mind, nor the obvious heart-hands gesture; it is a chosen gift, and it is the kind of thing that makes a stranger smile back.
